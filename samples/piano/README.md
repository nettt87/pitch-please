# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 (a Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm, licensed
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). These MP3s are the copies
Tone.js publishes at https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/, one per minor third
from A1 to C7. The app plays the nearest one to each note, shifted at most a semitone
and a half.
