# Guitar samples

A steel-string acoustic guitar, one recording per semitone from D2 to C7.

- **D2 to D5:** recorded by the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios
  ([Musical Instrument Samples](https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html)), edited by
  Nicholaus Brosowsky for [tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments)
  and released there under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
  The files here are cut losslessly, at an MP3 frame boundary, to their first 2.5 seconds.
  A2 is left out: that recording dies away as if muted, so the app plays A♯2 a semitone down.
- **D♯5 to C7:** the steel-string guitar of the Musyng Kite soundfont, as rendered to MP3 by
  [midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), released under
  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). These recordings
  remain under that license.
